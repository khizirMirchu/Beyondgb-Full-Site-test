Questions, answered

Common questions about planning, payments, enquiries and travel with BeyondGB.

Do you take online payments?

No. The website does not ask for card or payment details. Pricing and payment arrangements are discussed privately.

Can I customize a tour?

Yes. Customized planning is a core part of BeyondGB.

Do you work with international travelers?

Yes. Travelers from around the world can contact BeyondGB.

How does the enquiry form work?

You submit your basic trip details. The enquiry is stored by the website and can be connected to your email workflow before launch.

Can I use WhatsApp instead?

Yes. WhatsApp is available for direct trip discussions.

Are prices fixed on the website?

No. The website can show starting information, while final quotations can be handled privately.
